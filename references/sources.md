### Sources and References

This document lists the sources used in the analysis estimating parking volume and revenue at The University of British Columbia (UBC) Vancouver campus for 2025.

## 1. Parking Rates

**Source:** UBC Permits & rates

- **Publisher:** The University of British Columbia
- **URLs:** https://parking.ubc.ca/permits-rates, https://parking.ubc.ca/map
  - [Parking Permits and Rates](https://parking.ubc.ca/permits-rates)
  - [Parking Map](https://parking.ubc.ca/map)
- **Data used:** Published parking rates.
- **Purpose:** Make assumptions used in estimating parking revenue.
